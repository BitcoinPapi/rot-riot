ROT RIOT - assets that were not made for this game
===================================================

Everything else (all other pictures, all other sounds) is generated by the
game's own code. The sounds and ground photos below are free for any use,
also commercial, without credit (Creative Commons Zero, CC0); the owner
downloaded them from kenney.nl and polyhaven.com in November 2026.

    ui_click.ogg            click_001
    ui_move_1..3.ogg        tick_001, tick_002, tick_004
    ui_buy.ogg              confirmation_001
    ui_deny.ogg             error_001

  From "Impact Sounds" by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), CC0
    zombie_hit_1..5.ogg     impactSoft_medium_000..004
    boss_slam_1..5.ogg      impactPunch_heavy_000..004 (the boss hitting a wall)

PICTURES MADE WITH AN AI IMAGE GENERATOR  (assets/sprites/, from art_source/)

  Made by the game's owner with ChatGPT's image generator, from his own
  descriptions, then cut to size for the game:
    the hero JanJan (in a run and in the menus), the Runner, Brute, Chucker,
    Gasbag, Gobber, Lunger and Gang Fighter (and the Shambler, which is the
    Gang Fighter's picture without his machete), and the bosses of maps 1 to
    3 (November 2026) - the list is in art_source/README.txt
  The movement frames of the Gobber and of JanJan (walk, idle, attack, hit,
  death) were sent by the owner as sheets made from those same pictures
  (which tool made them is not written down yet - ask him before the Steam
  release, for the same declaration).
  (When the game goes on Steam, AI-made pictures have to be declared there.)

NOT IN THIS PROJECT, on purpose: recordings whose licence allows using them
in a game but not passing the files on (the Sonniss GDC bundle: a shotgun
and its bolt). They live only in the player's save folder, in "sfx"
(see the top of scripts/autoload/sfx.gd), never in the public project.

NOT USED (yet): "Top-down Shooter" by Kenney, CC0 - bright cartoon houses,
furniture and people. Kept out because its look does not fit the game's dark
one; pieces of it may serve the themed maps later, recoloured.
