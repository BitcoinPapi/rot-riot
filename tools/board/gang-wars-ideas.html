The story
  • You are Jean-Baptiste, "JanJan", a Haitian soldier.
  • The gangs bought a sorcerer's powder and send armies of the dead.
  • Each map is one gang's ground. Its leader is the boss, every 5th wave.
  • The gangs and leaders are invented, not real people.
Five maps, five bosses
  • Hill Camp: Kòk Batay
  • Cane Fields: Machwè Fè
  • Grand Cemetery: Papa Zo
  • National Road: Gwo Kamyon
  • The Docks: Wa Lannwit
  • 100 waves per map. All five still look alike, and so do the bosses.
Eight weapons
  • Pistol, SMG, shotgun
  • Machine gun, rifle
  • Launcher, flamethrower, chainsaw
  • Crates give a weapon for about 20 seconds. Two of the same: one in each hand.
Eight kinds of zombie
  • Shambler, Runner, Brute
  • Gobber (spits), Chucker (lobs rubble)
  • Gasbag (bursts), Lunger (leaps)
  • The gang boss
Growing stronger
  • Character level up to 100, nine skills
  • Weapon levels and upgrades
  • Gear in nine slots, five tiers, forging
  • Five pets, from level 18
  • A perk to choose after every boss (16 perks, for that run)
Playing together
  • Up to 3 players online, Mac and Windows
  • All gold and gear shared, zombies tougher
  • Fallen players can be picked up
